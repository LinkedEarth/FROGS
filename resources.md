## The Scientific Paper of the Future

The Scientific Paper of the Future ([SPF](https://scientificpaperofthefuture.org)) is an initiative to encourage (geo)scientists to publish papers together with the associated digital products of their research. 

An article reviewing best practices and author guidelines for a Geoscience Paper of the Future appears in the special issue of the [AGU Earth and Space Science journal](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/2015EA000136).

In addition to this article, updated training materials are available as a [PowerPoint presentation](https://figshare.com/articles/presentation/The_Scientific_Paper_of_the_Future_v2024_Training_Materials/25754244?file=46469986) and on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4OsbpIAknE).
