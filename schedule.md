# Overview schedule

The workshop will consist of four half-days (9am-12pm PST) of instruction followed by time to work on personal projects. Lectures will be recorded for aynchronous viewing on YouTube. Participants are expected to give a short presentation on the final day of the workshop. The presentations will not be made publicly available and will not be recorded.
